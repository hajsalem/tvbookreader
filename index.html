What it does

  • Narration with word highlightingYour device's own voice reads the book while the current word lights up. Pause and it resumes from the exact word.
  • Made for the remoteLeft and Right turn the page, Up and Down scroll a line, Center plays or pauses, and holding Center opens the menu.
  • Your library, your filesAdd books from device storage, a USB drive, or a home network share. On a TV, send books from your phone or computer over Wi-Fi.
  • Sync across devicesOptional: keep the same books and reading position on every TV and phone signed in to your Google account, through your own Google Drive.
  • Comfortable readingFour text sizes, four fonts, and five page colours including Sepia and Black, plus bookmarks, contents, and search.
  • Large PDFs tooScanned and image-heavy PDFs open as pages you can zoom and pan, with the PDF's own table of contents.

Free to read

Reading is always free. Narration is free for 7 days, then can be unlocked with a one-time purchase on Google Play. There's no subscription, no ads, and no tracking.

The app doesn't sell or include books. It opens the files you already have.
