TV Book Reader — Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026  ·  com.suliemanhs.tvbookreaderphone

TV Book Reader is an app for Android TV and Android phones that shows your EPUB, PDF, and TXT books and reads them aloud. This page explains, plainly, what the app needs access to and what it does with it.

Stays on your device

  • Your book files, unless you turn on Sync
  • Your network share address & password, if you add one
  • Bookmarks and reading settings
  • Your reading position, unless you turn on Sync

Never happens

  • No account is created by the app
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising
  • No selling or sharing of your data, and the developer never receives it

Local and USB storage access

To open your books, the app reads only the folders you choose with Android's own folder picker, on your device's storage or a connected USB drive. On older TVs without that picker, it uses a simple built-in folder browser with Android's standard storage permission. The app does not request access to all files on your device.

This access is used only to list and open the EPUB, PDF, and TXT files in those folders. Nothing is scanned beyond them, and nothing is sent off the device unless you turn on Sync.

Sending books over Wi-Fi (TV)

On a TV, Send over Wi-Fi lets you upload books from a phone or computer on your home network. While that screen is open, the TV shows a local address with a random code, and only devices on the same network that know it can send files. Books arrive directly from your device to the TV and are saved in the app's own storage. They don't pass through the internet or any server, and the TV stops accepting files when you leave the screen.

Network (SMB) shares

If you add a network share (a Windows/Samba/NAS folder), the app stores the host, share name, and any username/password you enter directly on the device, so it can reconnect automatically. That connection goes only to the address you provided. TV Book Reader doesn't operate any servers of its own.

These credentials are stored in ordinary app storage, not an encrypted vault. Treat this the way you'd treat any home‑network device: fine for a personal NAS on your own network, not a place to reuse a sensitive password.

Reading data

Your bookmarks, reading position, and display preferences (font, text size, page colour) are saved on your device so the app remembers your place. Bookmarks and preferences never leave the device. Your reading position leaves it only if you turn on Sync, described below. All of this is removed if you uninstall the app.

Sync across devices (optional)

Sync is off until you turn it on. When you do, the app asks you to choose a Google account and allow access to a private app folder in that account's Google Drive. The app can see only that folder, never your other Drive files. It stores:

  • copies of the book files on your device (EPUB, PDF, TXT), except books from network shares and files over 500 MB;
  • your reading position in each book and when you last opened it.

Every TV and phone where you turn on Sync with the same Google account gets the same books and picks up where you left off. The data stays in your own Google Drive and counts toward your Drive storage. The developer never receives it, it isn't shared with anyone else, and it isn't used for advertising or analytics. It travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The app doesn't create an account for you and doesn't store your name or email address.

Text‑to‑speech narration

Narration is performed by the text‑to‑speech engine already set up on your TV or phone (for example, Google's Speech Services). TV Book Reader hands the text being read to that engine. It does not operate its own speech service, and any processing beyond that is governed by your device's own speech engine and its settings.

Purchases

Reading is free. Narration can be unlocked with a one-time purchase, sold and processed entirely through Google Play's billing system. TV Book Reader never sees or stores your payment details, only the resulting purchase status, kept on your device, to know whether narration is unlocked.

Data sharing

TV Book Reader does not share, sell, or transmit your files, credentials, or reading activity to any third party. The only outbound network connections it makes are ones you set up or start: a network share you add, Google Play's billing service for purchases, and your own Google Drive if you turn on Sync.

Deleting your data

The app has no accounts, so there is nothing for the developer to delete. You control all of your data and can remove it yourself at any time.

Stop syncing

  1. Open TV Book Reader and select Sync on the bookshelf screen.
  2. Choose Turn off sync. Nothing more is uploaded from that device.

Delete everything Sync stored

  1. On a computer, open drive.google.com and sign in with the Google account you used for Sync.
  2. Open Settings (gear icon) → Settings → Manage apps.
  3. Find TV Book Reader, then choose Options → Delete hidden app data and confirm.

Remove the app's access to your Google account

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  2. Select TV Book Reader and choose Remove access.

Delete data on a TV or phone

  1. Uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → TV Book Reader → Storage → Clear storage.

Deleted data is removed immediately and isn't kept anywhere else. If you need help, email suliemanhs1993@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

TV Book Reader is a general‑audience reading tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
